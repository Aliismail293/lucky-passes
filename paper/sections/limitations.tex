\textbf{Small local models only.} We test three open models of 3B--8B parameters. Larger or hosted models memorise
more, so their memory-lucky share is likely larger; our numbers say nothing directly about them.

\textbf{Four datasets, English only.} Two public QA datasets, one invented corpus and one university calendar. The
invented corpus is small, so its rates have wide intervals. All text is English.

\textbf{Gold labels undercount retrieval.} A lucky pass under gold labels may be a retrieved non-gold passage that
also contains the answer \citep{cho2026rare}, especially in the large HotpotQA pool, where other paragraphs can mention
the same entity. The main rule (any gold passage) is a lower bound on lucky passes, but the label noise goes the
other way; the hand categorisation of lucky passes estimates how often this happens.

\textbf{An LLM judges correctness.} Answers that contains-match cannot decide are judged by a local LLM. The judge
was selected on reference labels from a stronger model (not human labels), on the same items it was then scored on,
so its agreement there is optimistic; the author's labels on a fresh sample give the unbiased estimate. The judge for
the \texttt{llama} generators (\texttt{qwen2.5:7b}) was selected on answers written by \texttt{qwen2.5:7b}, so its
accuracy may differ on \texttt{llama} answers.

\textbf{Generated questions.} Questions for the invented corpus and the calendar are written by
\texttt{qwen2.5:7b}, which is also a generator under test; questions it wrote may suit it. A second model from a
different family verifies each question, which limits but does not remove this.

\textbf{The prompt allows memory.} The RAG prompt asks the model to use the passages but does not forbid its own
knowledge, as in a typical application. A strict prompt would likely lower memory-lucky passes; we did not test it.

\textbf{Possible bad gold answers.} Some HotpotQA questions have gold answers that do not answer the question; two
such questions were flagged during the pilot and kept in the data.
